Data cleaner

Upload a messy Excel or CSV file, clean it with a few clicks, and download a neat Excel file. Your original file is never changed.

Clean a file in 5 steps

  1. Upload. Drag your CSV or Excel files into the upload box. Every sheet of an Excel file becomes its own table, shown as its own tab.
  2. Look at the preview. Check the table before you change anything.
  3. Pick an action. Above the preview you see groups of buttons (Structure, Values, Text, Rows, Summarise). Click one, for example Skip rows. A small window opens.
  4. Fill the window and click Apply. The preview changes at once. Each action you apply is saved as a numbered step. Wrong step? Use Reset to raw to start that table again.
  5. Download. Under Download, click Download cleaned workbook. All your tables come in one Excel file, one sheet per table.

What each action does

GroupActionUse it to
StructureSkip rowsThrow away title lines at the top (for example a company name and date above the real headings).
Empty rowsRemove rows with nothing in them.
Delete columnsRemove columns you don't need.
Rename columnChange a heading, for example "Amt" to "Amount".
ValuesColumn typesTell the app a column is a number, a date or text. For dates, you choose the format (dd-mm-yyyy etc.) and the app tells you if it fits.
Missing valuesFill blanks (with 0, a value you choose, the value above, the average) or drop those rows.
Numbers as textFix numbers like "1,25,000" that Excel keeps as text.
Round numbersRound to nearest, up or down, to the decimals you choose.
TextTrim whitespaceRemove extra spaces at the start and end.
Special charactersRemove symbols such as # or * from text.
Letter caseMake text UPPER, lower or Title Case.
Find & replaceChange one word with another, for example "PVT LTD" to "PRIVATE LIMITED".
RowsDuplicatesRemove repeated rows. You choose which columns decide a repeat, and whether to keep the first or the last.
Reset to rawRemove all steps of this table and go back to the uploaded file.
SummariseGroup & totalMake totals, for example party-wise total of amount.
PivotMake a cross-table, for example months across the top.
UnpivotTurn many month columns into rows (one column of months, one of values).
TipMany files with the same layout? Tick Apply to all loaded tables in the action window to do it once for every table.

Example: clean a party ledger

Your Excel has 3 title lines on top, extra spaces in party names, and amounts saved as text.

  1. Upload ledger.xlsx.
  2. Skip rows: 3, then Apply. The real headings now come first.
  3. Trim whitespace: tick the Party column, Apply.
  4. Numbers as text: tick Amount, Apply.
  5. Group & total: group by Party, total up Amount. Give the new table a name in Save as (for example "Party totals") and click Save summary. A new tab appears.
  6. Download cleaned workbook. You get both sheets, the clean ledger and Party totals.

Cleaning templates: do it in one click next month

At the top there is a Cleaning template box.

  • Finished cleaning? Click Save as template, give a name (for example "Receivables") and save. All steps of all files, and your summaries, are stored.
  • Next month, upload the new files, pick the template, and click Apply cleaning steps. The same cleaning runs again.
  • Show expected files tells you which files the template needs. If one is missing, Apply is refused until you upload it.
  • Update template replaces a saved template with what is on screen. The old version is not kept.
