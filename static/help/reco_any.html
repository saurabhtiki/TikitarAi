Reconcile Data

Match two Excel lists and see what agrees and what does not. For example your books against the bank, or your ledger against the party's ledger.

Steps

  1. Upload the Left and Right Excel files. The first sheet of each is used. Open Preview to check them.
  2. Replace Values file (optional). Names are often written differently ("PVT LTD" and "PRIVATE LIMITED"). This small Excel (columns REPLACE and REPLACE WITH) makes both sides look the same before matching. Click Download sample Replace Values file to see the format.
  3. Rounding-off tolerance. A difference up to this much (0 to 100, default 1) is treated as matched.
  4. Reconciliation method.
    • Sum Value: totals the amounts of each key on both sides, then compares the totals. Best when one side has many lines and the other has one.
    • Row Value: matches single rows one to one, within each key, if their values are within tolerance.
  5. Mapping Columns. The columns that say two lines are the same record (for example Invoice No and GST No). Choose how many pairs, then pick the Left column and the Right column of each pair.
  6. Reconciliation Columns. The amount columns to compare (must be numbers). Choose pairs the same way.
  7. Click Reconcile.

Reading the result

  • One tab per amount pair. It shows total on the Left, total on the Right, and the difference.
  • Reconciled = matched. Unreconciled = not matched, so look at these.
  • Open the previews to see the first 50 rows of each group.
  • Click Download Reconciliation Report for the full Excel with Summary, Reconciled and Unreconciled sheets.
