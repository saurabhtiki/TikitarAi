Dashboard

Arrange the answers you pinned in Chat with data into a neat report, then download it as HTML or Excel.

ImportantThis report lives only for this session. Download it before you close the tab or refresh the page.

Steps

  1. In Chat with data, click Pin to Dashboard under every answer you want. You can also pin whole loaded tables here with Pin a loaded table.
  2. Come to this page. Type the Report title (example: Q3 sales review). You can also add a logo.
  3. The view Build has two sides: your pinned items (the pool) and your report's sections.
  4. Click Add section and name it, for example Sales. You can add a subsection inside it with Add subsection.
  5. For each pinned item choose where it goes in Place in and click Place.
  6. Use the arrows to move items up and down. Use Show in columns with above to put two charts side by side.
  7. Add your own text with Add a block (notes, a picture such as an Excel chart, a link button).
  8. Switch to Preview & Download. Read the report from top to bottom. Choose a Style, or Customize style for fonts and colours.
  9. Click Download HTML (to email or open in a browser) or Download Excel (one sheet per item).

Example

You asked 3 questions in Chat with data: monthly sales, top 5 customers, region-wise sales. You pinned all three.

  1. Title: Sales report - October.
  2. Section Sales. Place "monthly sales" in it.
  3. Section Customers. Place "top 5 customers" and "region-wise sales" in it, and tick Show in columns with above on the second so they sit side by side.
  4. Add a block with one line of comment under each chart.
  5. Preview. Download HTML.
TipSave themes you like (Saved themes) and load them for the next report, so every report looks the same.
